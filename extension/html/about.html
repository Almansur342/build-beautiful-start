Overview

Research public website opportunities and manage leads in one workspace

Qrinux LeadLens helps agencies, freelancers, researchers, and sales teams scan websites, capture public contact evidence, review public SEO and technology signals, organize leads, and prepare focused outreach workflows.

Features

Core capabilities

Contact discovery

Public email, social profile, and phone evidence with source labels.

Website intelligence

SEO, technical, accessibility, content, policy, technology, robots.txt, sitemap, domain-age evidence, response headers, local performance observations, and contact-quality checks.

Browser CRM

Stage, priority, contacted status, follow-ups, country, industry, tags, notes, manual scores, and pain labels.

Bulk workflows

Resumable URL list, gradual controlled tabs, optional focus rotation, lightweight pagination, click-to-load details, filters, clipboard copy, XLSX export, and JSON backup.

Workflow

Scan, verify, organise, and export

  1. Scan one website from the page or paste a batch into Bulk Scanner.
  2. Expand a saved lead and manage CRM fields at the top of the card.
  3. Review public evidence before deciding whether outreach is appropriate.
  4. Copy reusable text, export filtered spreadsheet data, or download a local backup.
Storage

Local browser database

LeadLens stores saved leads and CRM updates inside the current Chrome profile. The lightweight lead index loads first; full evidence opens only when you expand a card. The bulk URL workspace is saved locally with no fixed queue cap, while only one to three controlled tabs scan at once. Interrupted queues can resume after Chrome is reopened. Storage warnings help you decide when to back up or clean older data.

Privacy

Local-first by design

Saved records remain in the browser database unless you intentionally copy, export, or download a backup. LeadLens processes public website evidence and does not guarantee sales, replies, meetings, or conversions.

Important notes

Use evidence responsibly

Some websites can block automated loading, require verification, or provide incomplete public information. Improvement, website-condition, contact-readiness, and outreach signals are transparent rule-based review aids—not verified facts or AI verdicts. Verify the business, contact path, evidence sources, accessibility findings, and applicable outreach requirements before contacting a lead.
