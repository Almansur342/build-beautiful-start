Qrinux LeadLens

Scan websites, capture public business evidence, and manage leads inside your browser.

1

Open and scan websites

LeadLens reads public page signals, email and social links, selected contact/about pages, SEO basics, and technology footprints from websites you choose to scan.

2

Review in Evidence Vault

Review lightweight website summaries first, then open a card to load full evidence only when needed. Manual CRM fields stay separate from transparent rule-based review aids, so users can see what was captured and what still needs verification.

3

Export and backup

Copy visible evidence, export a readable XLSX spreadsheet, download compact JSONL for external analysis, and keep complete JSON backups locally.

Local-first privacy

LeadLens does not use a remote database or paid API for your saved leads. Notes, tags, audits, accessibility preflight, content evidence, local performance observations, and backups stay in this browser unless you export them.
