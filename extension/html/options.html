Privacy

LeadLens stores discovery data locally in this browser.

Local browser storage Saved leads, notes, tags, and backups stay in Chrome extension storage unless you export them.

Evidence and review modes

LeadLens keeps captured website evidence separate from local rule-based review aids. No paid AI API or automatic AI verdict is used in this build.

No automatic AI verdicts LeadLens does not use a local language model to assign final country, industry, qualification, contact validity, recommended service, or sales decisions. This prevents unverified model output from changing captured facts.
Evidence-first workflow LeadLens captures public contacts, phones, social profiles, business schema, main content, policy and service links, dates, domain age, technology stack, SEO metadata, accessibility preflight, performance observations, route evidence, headers, robots.txt, sitemap, and safety flags.
Local model safety boundary No local language model is active in this release. Raw evidence, contacts, location evidence, technologies, measurements, and exports never depend on model-generated decisions.

Blacklist websites

Domains listed here are ignored by scanning and contact capture. The recommended list blocks major social platforms, marketplaces, directories, hosted-profile platforms, large enterprise brands, and major web-development or SEO agencies that are not normal outreach prospects.

Backup and cleanup

Lead records are stored in this Chrome profile database. Export JSON backups regularly, restore backups when needed, or remove old leads.

Full system reset

Use this only when you want to start from a completely clean LeadLens installation. This removes all saved leads, contacts, SEO evidence, CRM notes, follow-up dates, browser-database records, cached technologies, custom blacklist edits, and local settings.

Irreversible action Download a backup first when you need to keep existing records.
